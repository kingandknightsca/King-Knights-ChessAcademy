# Clear Staunton chess pieces

Source: [Lichess Chessnut SVG set](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/chessnut) by Alexis Luengas. The [Lichess copying notice](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md) identifies this set as Apache License 2.0.

The six light-side SVGs have their white `#fff` fills changed to light gold `#f1ca78` for King & Knights Chess Academy. The six black-side SVGs are unmodified. All 12 SVG files remain separate from application code. The original three-dimensional prototype assets are retained for recovery and comparison.
