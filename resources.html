Support at every step

For learners, parents, and teachers.

01

Student homework

Each lesson has six multiple-choice questions and five practical board activities. Young Minds homework is designed for a 14-day submission window between bi-weekly classes.

02

Parent support

Plain-language explanations and separate answers help parents support a child, even without previous chess experience.

03

Instructor guidance

A separate marking guide supports consistent feedback while keeping answers out of student assignments.

Class homework and support materials are supplied through the academy. The published book is available separately in paperback and Kindle editions.
